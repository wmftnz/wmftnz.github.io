The HQ

Unit 20, a warehouse that runs itself.

Warehouse, workshop, event space and bookable studios. It's the base every show packs out of, and the proving ground for everything I build: customers schedule their own pickups and the door unlocks itself with a time-boxed code, labels print unattended in the workshop, and a wall display shows the crew what's leaving today.

Self-service dry hire. Pick a time online, get a door code by email, collect your gear. A customer can pick up at 7am on a Sunday with nobody on site.
Unit 20 Studios. A DJ practice studio booked online at studio.unit20.nz. Pay, verify your ID once, and your door code arrives automatically.
Unit 20 Live. Ticketing for our own shows at unit20.nz, built with Joseph Hendry. Card payments, QR check-in, pre-sale ballots and a full admin dashboard, with $54,000+ in payments processed to date.
One shared system. The studio and the AV company share one database, so the studio calendar automatically refuses bookings on days the decks are out on a show.

Who's on, live
Doors that open themselves
Customers pick their own pickup and return time online, then get a time-boxed smart-lock door code by email. The lock validates it offline, and nobody needs to be on site. This one feature removed staffed handovers entirely.
A client pack for every job
Clients see their timings, runsheet and venue plan, answer questions the crew has posted, and upload their own files. The whole job lives in one shared place instead of an email chain.
Venue plans from a photo
Upload a photo of any venue's floor plan, set the scale, and lay the show out to the metre. Works on a phone on site, and it's shared with crew and client.
Runsheets and scheduling
Plan the show timeline once and share it with everyone. Crew see their shifts, accept or decline by email, and the week planner builds itself from the jobs.
Quote to invoice on one record
Quote a job, convert the accepted quote into a live booking, invoice it and track what has been paid. Nothing gets re-typed along the way.
One system for everything
Every piece of gear is tracked across overlapping jobs, and unit20 studio bookings surface in the same calendar, so the two businesses can never double-book each other.
